## Selected Projects

**Explainable ML for Sentiment Analysis** — Built machine learning classifiers for sentiment analysis and applied LIME explainability techniques to understand model predictions. Evaluated the fidelity of explanations by removing influential words. [Code](https://github.com/shantanu778/semantic_explainable_analyzer)

**Pre-trained vs. Randomly Initialized Models** — Comparative study of pre-trained (ResNet-34, BERT) and randomly initialized deep learning models on image classification (cassava leaf disease) and NLP (COVID tweet sentiment). Demonstrated the importance of transfer learning. [Code](https://github.com/AbhishekRS4/Deep_Learning)

**Offline Handwriting Recognition** — Evaluated deep neural network approaches for digitizing offline handwritten text on the Dead Sea Scrolls and IAM datasets. Achieved 5% character error rates using ARU-net, CNN, and encoder-decoder architectures. [Paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZLKMhI-aQS9EtKKoM961NYxakvSpPV6d/view?usp=sharing)

**Chest X-ray Abnormality Detection** — Ensemble of deep CNNs for localizing abnormalities in chest X-ray images, published at the International Conference on Advanced Technologies for Communications (ATC 2021).
